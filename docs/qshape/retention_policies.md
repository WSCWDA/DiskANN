# P3.1-B
Policies none and bounded retain previous behavior. Dynamic policies use 64 fixed byte quotas (sum equals READY budget), shard-local LRU and publication-order expiry lists. TTL is fixed from publication, lazily reclaimed on shard lookup/publication; no background timer. TTL rejects on full quota; LRU evicts oldest accessed; ttl-lru purges expired then evicts LRU. Large regions exceeding shard quota reject. No cross-shard borrowing. This differs from bounded global first-fill; capacity fragmentation is a confound to measure. Live payload reservations remain charged until final reference release. No cache-only yet.
READY_STATS=1 enables hit age, unused eviction and producer-group attribution. Group means execution-position group, not synchronized batch. Evictions attributed to triggering query, not producer. Age sums divide by corresponding hit/eviction counts. Unused eviction excludes final teardown. Retention timing includes lookup/purge and admission, not copy or lock acquisition. Diagnostics off by default; run separately. Breakdown may coexist, reuse history may not.
Run test_ready_policies.cpp and previous tests. run_retention.sh executes four searches per policy/repeat including off controls (100 total for five repeats). validate_retention.py verifies hashes, logical counters, budgets and policy accounting; outputs retention_metrics.csv and retention_groups.csv. Use fresh OUT, READY_TTL_US=5000, READY_BUDGET=16777216, REPEATS=5. TTL tests use real clock with sleep; not deterministic under extreme scheduling. No performance claim until target tests.
